# 🚗 Toll Plaza Revenue Analysis using Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Toll Plaza Revenue Analysis** project is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze toll plaza revenue, vehicle traffic, payment methods, and revenue loss across India.

The dashboard helps understand revenue performance, traffic patterns, vehicle categories, and differences between expected and collected revenue.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to transform toll plaza transaction data into meaningful business insights.

The analysis focuses on:

* Revenue performance across states and toll plazas
* Vehicle traffic patterns
* Payment method analysis
* Expected vs collected revenue
* Revenue loss
* Traffic patterns by time and month
* Toll exemption analysis

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1️⃣ Overview

Provides a high-level view of toll plaza performance.

**Key analysis:**

* Total Revenue
* Expected Revenue
* Collected Revenue
* Revenue by State
* Revenue by Payment Method
* Revenue by Month
* Revenue by Toll Plaza

### 2️⃣ Revenue Analysis

Focuses on revenue performance and collection patterns.

**Key analysis:**

* Revenue by Vehicle Type
* Revenue by Shift
* Expected vs Collected Revenue
* Revenue by Highway
* Holiday vs Non-Holiday Revenue

### 3️⃣ Vehicle Analysis

Analyzes vehicle traffic patterns across toll plazas.

**Key analysis:**

* Vehicle Count by State
* Vehicle Count by Time Slot
* Vehicle Count by Month
* Toll Exemption Analysis

### 4️⃣ Revenue Loss Analysis

Focuses on identifying revenue loss patterns.

**Key analysis:**

* Revenue Loss by State
* Revenue Loss by Toll Plaza
* Revenue Loss by Month
* Revenue by Vehicle Type
